# 2026-10-31 compatibility work log

Historical engineering notes; later commits supersede some details. This file is not the current product specification.

## Android 4.0 rewrite

compat was created from origin/pro, committed as `843deb6` and pushed. Manifest merging failed at minSdk 14/21/23 because the then-used `material3-ripple:1.5.0-beta01` required 24; lowering one number could not solve it. Compose UI was replaced with framework View/Canvas while retaining calendar engine, contracts, persistence and words.

New components included `PlainDate` (no java.time/desugaring on API 14; desugaring below 21 required multidex), `Plate`/`PageArtwork` (360×600 and 640×360, eleven themes), `PaperView` (Camera page turns), `ChooserView` (Scroller), initial `StoreActivity` (three columns and DragEvent ordering), `StyleSheetDialog` and `Ui` workers/preview cache. The store was subsequently removed.

Compatibility replacements: ImageDecoder to BitmapFactory/ExifInterface; AtomicFile to temp/fsync/rename; coroutines/Flow to Handler/listeners; tint to ColorFilter; getDrawable to resources.getDrawable; vectors to generated-density PNGs; pre-26 raster launchers. Midnight became a common page turn; shared-element and wavy progress were omitted. Local unit/Lint/build passed, APK minSdk was 14 and dex contained no Compose/coroutines/java.time references. Push CI builds the APK.

## Same-device comparison

`run-as` calendar preferences drove matching dates/themes/fullscreen on emulator-5554, with pro built separately. Pixel differences located a portrait Panels error: hanging/almanac had incorrectly used side-by-side columns, showing 10.1%/9.2% difference, reduced to 6.2%/6.7% after correction. Compose Print defaults to SansSerif; only explicit Good Day/Verse/Almanac and Hanging lunar lines use Serif. An accidental serif default affected four themes.

Android 15 edge-to-edge required top/bottom window insets. The sheet adopted content height, rounded top corners and a right-aligned Done action; chooser controls align to the preview right edge. Built-ins became nonremovable/nonreorderable after store/delete-gesture removal.

## Blank chooser and indicators

On reported OPPO PHY110 / Android 15, pages after the first showed only background while fullscreen worked. `View.scrollTo` already shifted Canvas by `-scrollX`, and drawing subtracted it again. HWUI optimized drawColor into the background, leaving only color visible. A private scroll offset replaced View scrolling.

A 10 MB preview could not keep three pages in a 24 MB LRU, causing repeated render/eviction. Width capped at 900 px (about 5.8 MB each) allowed three cached previews and idle drawing stopped. Fast flings previously added displacement and velocity pages; now they move one page from gesture origin. A v23 light-status flag on Holo's black bar made icons invisible and was removed.

The inherited maximum-nine sliding indicator window looked static for pages 1–5 of eleven. It became one indicator per theme, 4 dp dots and 16 dp selected capsule with adaptive gaps; measured eleven-dot width was 261 px, centered with correct fourth-page highlight.

## Settings, gestures and wide screens

uiautomator opened all eleven sheets and checked animation/interaction storage, traditional ink/special-edition/dark/schedule/time-picker/ambient controls, and image fill/picker. GestureDetector could report a long press before a horizontal swipe, exiting fullscreen; manual DOWN/UP displacement/time tracking replaced it.

Traditional date numerals fit the free band between side columns through `dayNumber(day, bandLeft, bandRight, top, height, cap)`, leaving 56 design units and allowing narrowing but no unwanted widening. 16:9, 18:9, 21:9 and landscape showed complete balanced digits. Return to Today moved from 28 to 72 dp to avoid bottom lunar text.

## Small-screen sheet crash

At 720×1280, traditional sheets exceeded the 82% height cap and crashed, though 1440×2560 did not. `ViewGroup.LayoutParams` could not be cast to `MarginLayoutParams` during DecorView measurement. `FrameLayout.LayoutParams` replaced it; height limits moved to `window.setLayout()` and `fitToContent()` after updates. Sheets opened at 720×1280, 1080×2160 and 1440×2560, could scroll to Done and saved dark changes.

## Ambient policy and date size

The original 10–40 lx dead band kept a dark page dark around 25 lx. compat intentionally adopted one threshold: below 10 lx dark for 30 seconds, at least 10 lx light for 10 seconds, with tests. `Plate.bigDay` formerly used `min(w*.72, h*.9)` and sometimes negative height, producing small/missing digits. It now fits measured “28” glyphs in both dimensions and callers clamp height to at least 80.

All four traditional defaults use weekend/festival red and otherwise green, with explicit user choice taking priority. The peitaiyi-specific webRed path was removed. Special-edition help is renderer-specific and does not mention Shihe in other themes.
